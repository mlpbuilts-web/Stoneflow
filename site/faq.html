Questions, answered

Are the slabs on your website really in stock?

Yes. The slab list reads our live inventory, the same one our sales team uses, so you see what's available right now.

What do -J, -SJ and -R mean?

They're slab sizes: R is regular, J is jumbo and SJ is super jumbo. Larger slabs mean fewer seams on long counters and islands.

What is a lot number, and why does it matter?

A lot is a batch of slabs cut from the same block. Slabs from one lot match each other, so ask for the lot number when you choose your stone.

What does bookmatched mean?

Bookmatched slabs are cut so their veins mirror each other across a seam, like the pages of an open book.
