Quartz

Engineered quartz is hard-wearing, non-porous and low-maintenance, with the look of marble. Most of our stock is Calacatta and Carrara styles, plus printed full-body quartz, whose pattern runs through the slab.

Sizes and thickness

  • Regular: about 120–127 in long. Fine for most kitchens and baths.
  • Jumbo (-J): about 126–131 in. Fewer seams on long runs and islands.
  • Super jumbo (-SJ): the largest slabs, for big islands without a seam.
  • Thickness: 3 cm is standard. 2 cm is lighter and suits vanities and backsplashes.

Bookmatched slabs

Bookmatched (BM) slabs are cut so the veins mirror each other across a seam. Ask for consecutive slabs from the same lot so your kitchen matches.
